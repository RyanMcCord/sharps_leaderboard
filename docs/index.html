Who's actually making money on their free picks.

Units are the amount risked, so 1 unit on a -110 winner is +0.91. When a post doesn't show odds, -110 is assumed. ROI is units won divided by units risked.

Faded accounts have fewer than 30 graded picks in the selected range, which is too few to read much into. Player props, partial-game bets (like first-half lines) and other picks the tracker can't grade automatically aren't counted until they're graded by hand.

Picks posted after their game started are marked late and left out of the standings. Deleted picks still count: the tracker keeps a copy of every post.
